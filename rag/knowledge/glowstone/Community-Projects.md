Here is a list of community projects that have specific support for Glowstone, or are examples of how people are using Glowstone for awesome projects.

* [Aternos](https://aternos.org) - Free Minecraft servers for all.
* [ProtocolSupport](https://protocol.support/) - Support older Minecraft versions on the latest server version.
* [GlowNPC](https://github.com/DenAbr/GlowNPC) - Glowstone plugin demonstration of creating NPCs (Citizens proof of concept alternative)
* [gsredis](https://github.com/momothereal/gsredis) - A Glowstone plugin to replace file-system world storage with Redis

Have your own cool project related to Glowstone? Add it to the list!
